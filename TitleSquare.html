How to play

Normal: one step, then it turns black Pink: two steps (turns into a normal square after the first) Green: always safe, never runs out Black: stepping on it loses and restarts the level

Clear every normal and pink square to win. Move with arrow keys / WASD, swipe, or tap a neighbouring square. Z = undo, R = restart.
